Welcome Home

A place to know God and be known.

Umoja P.A.G Church is a family church of distinction that exists to worship God in truth and Spirit, proclaim the Word of God, and advance the Kingdom of God through evangelism, discipleship, social and economic programmes and capacity building through the power of the Holy Spirit in Kenya and beyond.
