New TV Screens & Upgraded Camera

We're installing new display screens around the sanctuary and upgrading our camera equipment, so every seat has a clear view of worship and the Word, and our livestream reaches the same quality as what's happening in the room.

So far the new screens have been sourced and camera equipment selected; installation and wiring are currently under way. Final calibration and livestream testing are the remaining steps before this is fully complete.

Ongoing — 80%

Hall Completion Project

Our church hall is becoming a spacious, modern venue for Sunday services, fellowships, weddings, and community gatherings — a space the whole congregation can be proud of and put to use throughout the week.

At about 80% complete, the structure, roofing, and flooring are done. What remains is finishing work — fittings, final interior touches, and furnishing — before the hall is ready to open for full use.

Completed — 100%

Gate & Cabros Project

This project covered a new gate, boundary fencing, and cabros paving across the compound — improving security, safe access for members and children, and giving visitors a welcoming first impression of the church grounds.

This project is fully complete and already in daily use by the congregation. Thank you to everyone who gave toward making it happen.
