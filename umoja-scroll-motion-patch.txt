<h1 class="section-title">Ministries</h1>
<p class="section-intro">You belong here. Whatever your gifts, age, or season of life, there's a family below waiting to welcome you in — explore each ministry, meet the leaders, and see where God might be calling you to serve and grow.</p>
</div>

<h2 id="cell-groups-title">Cell Groups</h2>
<div class="verse-block">
<p class="verse-text">For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them.</p>
<p class="verse-ref">Matthew 18:20</p>
</div>
<p>Local neighbourhood fellowships that meet weekly for prayer, Bible study and mutual care.</p>
<p class="min-desc-more">Cell groups are the heartbeat of our church family — smaller circles where you're known by name, not just a face in the crowd. Each group works through the same weekly study so the whole church grows together, while making room for the prayer requests, testimonies, and everyday life of the people in the room.</p>
<div class="sub-list">
<strong>Neighbourhoods:</strong> Jericho, Nazareth, Bethlehem, Judah, Galilee, Jerusalem
          </div>
<div class="expect-title">What to expect</div>
<ul class="expect-list">
<li>Opening prayer and worship, followed by a guided Bible study</li>
<li>Open sharing time — testimonies, prayer requests, practical support</li>
<li>Tea/refreshments and fellowship after the study</li>
<li>A group leader who follows up with you during the week</li>
</ul>
<div class="info-panel">
<div class="info-row"><strong>Meets</strong><span>Weekly — confirm the current day and time with the Cell Groups Coordinator</span></div>
<div class="info-row"><strong>Where</strong><span>Hosted at a cell member's home (varies by neighbourhood)</span></div>
<div class="info-row"><strong>Who</strong><span>Open to all — members and first-time visitors welcome</span></div>
<div class="info-row"><strong>To join</strong><span>Pick the neighbourhood closest to you and reach out via the How to Join section on the home page; a leader will get in touch</span></div>
</div>
<div class="leader">
<div class="leader-photo no-photo"></div>
<div class="leader-body">
<p class="leader-name">Mary Wanjiru</p>
<p class="leader-role">Cell Groups Coordinator</p>
<p class="leader-msg">"Cell groups are where life happens — come as you are; we will pray, study, and grow together."</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="btn-row">
<a class="btn-primary" href="index.html#join">Join a cell group</a>

<h2 id="worship-team-title">Worship Team</h2>
<div class="verse-block">
<p class="verse-text">Serve the LORD with gladness: come before his presence with singing.</p>
<p class="verse-ref">Psalm 100:2</p>
</div>
<p>Leading the church into God's presence each Sunday through music and worship leadership.</p>
<p class="min-desc-more">Whether you sing, play an instrument, or run sound from the booth, the worship team exists to help the whole congregation focus on God in every service. New members go through a short onboarding period alongside the team before joining a Sunday rotation, so you're never thrown in unprepared.</p>
<div class="expect-title">What to expect</div>
<ul class="expect-list">
<li>Weekly rehearsal to prepare the set list for the coming Sunday(s)</li>
<li>A rotation schedule so serving doesn't clash with your other commitments</li>
<li>Mentorship from experienced vocalists and musicians</li>
<li>Opportunities to grow in both skill and spiritual leadership</li>
</ul>
<div class="info-panel">
<div class="info-row"><strong>Rehearsals</strong><span>Saturdays, 3:00 PM</span></div>
<div class="info-row"><strong>Serves</strong><span>Rotates across both Sunday services</span></div>
<div class="info-row"><strong>Who</strong><span>Vocalists, instrumentalists, and sound/tech support</span></div>
<div class="info-row"><strong>Requirements</strong><span>A short conversation with the Worship Pastor — no formal audition required</span></div>
</div>
<div class="leader">
<div class="leader-photo no-photo"></div>
<div class="leader-body">
<p class="leader-name">Alex</p>
<p class="leader-role">Worship Pastor</p>
<p class="leader-msg">"We aim to lead worship that opens hearts to encounter Jesus — musicians and vocalists are welcome."</p>
</div>

<h2 id="choir-title">Choir</h2>
<div class="verse-block">
<p class="verse-text">O sing unto the LORD a new song: sing unto the LORD, all the earth.</p>
<p class="verse-ref">Psalm 96:1</p>
</div>
<p>Choral ministry for special services and congregational encouragement.</p>
<p class="min-desc-more">The choir ministers through song at special services, festive celebrations, and select Sundays throughout the year — bringing harmony, testimony, and encouragement to the congregation. Members rehearse together to learn parts and build the kind of unity that makes choral singing come alive.</p>
<div class="expect-title">What to expect</div>
<ul class="expect-list">
<li>Weekly rehearsals to learn and practise new pieces</li>
<li>Vocal part training (soprano, alto, tenor, bass) for all experience levels</li>
<li>Ministering at designated services and special church events</li>
<li>A welcoming space to grow your voice and your faith together</li>
</ul>
<div class="info-panel">
<div class="info-row"><strong>Rehearsals</strong><span>Ahead of each ministering Sunday — confirm the day with the Choir Director</span></div>
<div class="info-row"><strong>Ministers</strong><span>Selected Sundays and special services throughout the year</span></div>
<div class="info-row"><strong>Who</strong><span>Open to all voice ranges and experience levels</span></div>
<div class="info-row"><strong>Requirements</strong><span>No prior choir experience needed — just a willing heart to learn</span></div>
</div>

<div class="leader-photo no-photo"></div>

<h2 id="media-team-title">Media Team</h2>
<div class="verse-block">
<p class="verse-text">Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature.</p>
<p class="verse-ref">Mark 16:15</p>
</div>
<p>Sound, visuals, and streaming — help us communicate the message clearly online and in-house.</p>
<p class="min-desc-more">From live sound mixing to camera work, lyric slides, and streaming to YouTube and Facebook, the media team makes sure every service is heard and seen clearly — in the sanctuary and by those watching from home. It's hands-on, practical serving that suits anyone who enjoys tech, photography, or design.</p>
<div class="expect-title">What to expect</div>
<ul class="expect-list">
<li>Hands-on training on sound desk, cameras, and streaming software</li>
<li>A rotation across roles — sound, visuals/slides, live-stream, photography</li>
<li>Arriving early on serving days to set up and test equipment</li>
<li>A team environment where technical and creative skills both matter</li>
</ul>
<div class="info-panel">
<div class="info-row"><strong>Setup call time</strong><span>Before the assigned service or event — confirm the rota with the Media Lead</span></div>
<div class="info-row"><strong>Serves</strong><span>Both Sunday services and special events</span></div>
<div class="info-row"><strong>Who</strong><span>Anyone interested in sound, video, photography, or streaming</span></div>
<div class="info-row"><strong>Requirements</strong><span>No experience required — training provided</span></div>
</div>

</div>
<p>Age-appropriate Bible teaching for children and teens delivered by caring volunteers.</p>
<p class="min-desc-more">Children and teens are split into age-appropriate classes so lessons, songs, and activities always match where they're at. Every teacher and helper is there because they love seeing young people meet Jesus in a way that's fun, safe, and easy for parents to trust during the service.</p>
<div class="expect-title">What to expect</div>
<ul class="expect-list">
<li>Age-graded classes with Bible stories, songs, and simple activities</li>

<li>Occasional special programs and holiday activities</li>
</ul>

<div class="info-row"><strong>Who</strong><span>Children and teens of church members and visitors</span></div>

UMOJA P.A.G CHURCH — SCROLL IMAGE MOTION PATCH

1. Paste the CSS block at the very end of index.css.
2. Paste the JavaScript block at the very end of index.js.
3. The JavaScript automatically detects the existing image wrappers in both index.html and ministries.html, so no HTML edits are required for the effect to work.
4. Save the files and hard-refresh with Ctrl+F5.

================ CSS ================

/* ================================================================
   REPLAYABLE IMAGE POP ON SCROLL
   Used by index.html and ministries.html. Each marked image rises
   smoothly into place whenever it re-enters the viewport, including
   when a visitor scrolls back upward through the page.
   ================================================================ */
.scroll-pop-media {
  --scroll-pop-distance: 54px;
  --scroll-pop-delay: 0ms;
  --scroll-pop-duration: 900ms;

  opacity: 0;
  transform: translate3d(0, var(--scroll-pop-distance), 0) scale(0.945);
  transform-origin: 50% 72%;
  filter: blur(7px) saturate(0.86);
  backface-visibility: hidden;
  will-change: transform, opacity, filter;
}

.scroll-pop-media.is-in-view {
  opacity: 1;
  transform: translate3d(0, 0, 0) scale(1);
  filter: blur(0) saturate(1);
  animation: scrollImagePopUp var(--scroll-pop-duration)
    cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) var(--scroll-pop-delay) both;
}

@keyframes scrollImagePopUp {
  0% {
    opacity: 0;
    transform: translate3d(0, var(--scroll-pop-distance), 0) scale(0.945);
    filter: blur(7px) saturate(0.86);
  }

  60% {
    opacity: 1;
    transform: translate3d(0, -8px, 0) scale(1.015);
    filter: blur(0) saturate(1);
  }

  80% {
    opacity: 1;
    transform: translate3d(0, 3px, 0) scale(0.997);
    filter: blur(0) saturate(1);
  }

  100% {
    opacity: 1;
    transform: translate3d(0, 0, 0) scale(1);
    filter: blur(0) saturate(1);
  }
}

@media (max-width: 640px) {
  .scroll-pop-media {
    --scroll-pop-distance: 36px;
    --scroll-pop-duration: 760ms;
  }
}

@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) {
  .scroll-pop-media,
  .scroll-pop-media.is-in-view {
    opacity: 1;
    transform: none;
    filter: none;
    animation: none !important;
    will-change: auto;
  }
}

// ── REPLAYABLE IMAGE POP ON SCROLL ───────────────────────────────
// Both index.html and ministries.html use this module. Elements marked
// with data-scroll-pop rise smoothly into view and reset only after they
// have completely left the viewport, so the motion also replays when the
// visitor scrolls upward.
(() => {
  "use strict";

  const targetSelector = [
    "[data-scroll-pop]",
    ".about-img-main",
    ".pastor-media",
    ".leadership-photo",
    ".ministry-card-media",
    ".giving-media",
    ".event-card-media",
    ".min-card > img",
  ].join(",");
  const reducedMotion = window.matchMedia("(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)");
  const registered = new WeakSet();
  let imageObserver = null;

  function resolvedGridColumnCount(group) {
    if (!group) return 1;

    const template = window.getComputedStyle(group).gridTemplateColumns;
    if (!template || template === "none") return 1;

    return Math.max(1, template.split(/\s+/).filter(Boolean).length);
  }

  function staggerDelayFor(element) {
    const group = element.closest(
      ".ministry-grid, .leadership-grid, .events-grid, .min-list",
    );

    if (!group || group.classList.contains("min-list")) return 0;

    const items = Array.from(group.querySelectorAll(targetSelector));
    const index = Math.max(0, items.indexOf(element));
    const columns = Math.min(resolvedGridColumnCount(group), 4);

    return (index % columns) * 80;
  }

  function showWithoutAnimation(element) {
    element.classList.add("scroll-pop-media", "is-in-view");
  }

  function registerElement(element) {
    if (!(element instanceof HTMLElement) || registered.has(element)) return;

    registered.add(element);
    element.classList.add("scroll-pop-media");
    element.style.setProperty(
      "--scroll-pop-delay",
      `${staggerDelayFor(element)}ms`,
    );

    if (reducedMotion.matches || !imageObserver) {
      showWithoutAnimation(element);
    } else {
      imageObserver.observe(element);
    }
  }

  function registerWithin(root = document) {
    if (root instanceof Element && root.matches(targetSelector)) {
      registerElement(root);
    }

    if (root.querySelectorAll) {
      root.querySelectorAll(targetSelector).forEach(registerElement);
    }
  }

  function createObserver() {
    if (!("IntersectionObserver" in window) || reducedMotion.matches) {
      imageObserver = null;
      return;
    }

    imageObserver = new IntersectionObserver(
      (entries) => {
        entries.forEach((entry) => {
          if (entry.isIntersecting) {
            entry.target.classList.add("is-in-view");
          } else {
            // The element is now outside the viewport. Removing the class
            // prepares it to animate again on either upward or downward re-entry.
            entry.target.classList.remove("is-in-view");
          }
        });
      },
      {
        threshold: 0.01,
        rootMargin: "0px",
      },
    );
  }

  function applyMotionPreference() {
    imageObserver?.disconnect();

    if (reducedMotion.matches || !("IntersectionObserver" in window)) {
      imageObserver = null;
      document.querySelectorAll(targetSelector).forEach(showWithoutAnimation);
      return;
    }

    createObserver();

    document.querySelectorAll(targetSelector).forEach((element) => {
      element.classList.add("scroll-pop-media");
      element.classList.remove("is-in-view");
      imageObserver.observe(element);
    });
  }

  createObserver();
  registerWithin(document);

  // Event cards are rebuilt when a filter changes. This watches for newly
  // inserted marked media and applies the same effect automatically.
  const contentObserver = new MutationObserver((mutations) => {
    mutations.forEach((mutation) => {
      mutation.addedNodes.forEach((node) => {
        if (node instanceof Element) registerWithin(node);
      });
    });
  });

  contentObserver.observe(document.body, {
    childList: true,
    subtree: true,
  });

  if (typeof reducedMotion.addEventListener === "function") {
    reducedMotion.addEventListener("change", applyMotionPreference);
  } else if (typeof reducedMotion.addListener === "function") {
    reducedMotion.addListener(applyMotionPreference);
  }
})();
