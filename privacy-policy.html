Privacy Policy

This notice explains what information Umoja P.A.G Church collects through this website, why it is used, who may receive it, and how you can exercise your rights.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who This Notice Covers

This notice applies to information submitted through the Umoja P.A.G Church website. Umoja P.A.G Church determines why that information is collected and how it is used for church communication, event administration, prayer support, and pastoral care.

2. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on the form you choose to use:

  • Event registration: first and last name, phone number, optional email address, optional age group and area or estate, the selected event, practical notes, consent, and confirmation that the registrant is an adult or has parent or guardian permission.
  • Contact messages: name, email address, subject, message, and consent to receive a response.
  • Prayer requests: name, the prayer request, and consent for the prayer team to receive it.
  • Pastoral-care requests: name, email address, the care request, and consent for authorised pastoral staff to respond.

Prayer and pastoral-care submissions may contain sensitive information about beliefs, health, family, or personal circumstances. Please share only what is necessary and avoid submitting another person’s private information without their permission.

Each form also contains an invisible anti-spam field. It is not intended for ordinary visitors and should remain blank.

3. Why We Use the Information

We use submitted information only for the purpose shown next to the form, including:

  • processing an event registration and communicating event details;
  • responding to a question, testimony, or general message;
  • sharing a prayer request with authorised prayer-team members;
  • arranging pastoral follow-up, counselling, or a requested visit;
  • preventing spam, misuse, and duplicate submissions; and
  • meeting legal, safeguarding, and accountability obligations.

We do not sell personal information or use form submissions for unrelated advertising.

4. Consent and Your Choices

The website asks for consent before a form can be submitted. You may choose not to submit a form and instead contact the church by phone, email, or in person. You may withdraw consent for future use by contacting us, although this does not affect processing that was lawful before the withdrawal.

5. Children and Young People

A visitor under 18 should submit an event registration only with permission from a parent or guardian. The website is not intended to collect information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child’s information was submitted without appropriate permission, contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, remove it.

6. Who May Access the Information

Access should be limited to authorised church staff, the prayer team, pastoral staff, or a ministry or event leader who needs the information for the stated purpose. Website hosting, email-delivery, or other technical service providers may process information on the church’s behalf when required to operate the service. They should receive only the access necessary for that function.

The front-end website does not save failed form submissions or prayer and pastoral-care details in your browser’s local storage.

7. Retention

Information should be kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, for ongoing pastoral support, or to meet a legal or safeguarding obligation. When it is no longer needed, it should be deleted, anonymised, or securely archived under the church’s retention procedures. You may ask us to review or delete your information.

8. Security

We take reasonable organisational and technical steps to restrict access and protect submitted information. The deployed website should use HTTPS, server-side validation, access controls, and appropriate safeguards for sensitive requests. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. External Services

This site uses or links to third-party services:

  • Google Fonts supplies the website typefaces and may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details.
  • Google Maps is loaded only after you select “Load Interactive Map.”
  • cdnjs supplies the QR-code library only when you open the registration QR tool.
  • YouTube and Facebook are external links. Their own privacy terms apply after you follow those links.

The supplied website does not include an analytics service, advertising tracker, or marketing pixel. Third-party services may nevertheless use their own technical storage or logs under their policies.

10. Your Data-Protection Rights

Under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019, you may have rights to be informed, access personal data, object to processing, request correction or deletion, request restriction, receive portable data where applicable, and complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

You can learn more from the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.
