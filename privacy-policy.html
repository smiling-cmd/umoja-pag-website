Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Umoja P.A.G Church collects through this website, why, and how you can ask us about it.

Last updated: July 2026

Information We Collect

When you use our registration form, we ask for:

  • First and last name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Age group and area/estate (optional)
  • What you're registering for (new membership, a ministry, or a cell group)
  • Any notes, questions, or prayer requests you choose to share

We don't use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts on this site, so we don't collect browsing data about your visit.

How We Use Your Information

We use what you submit only to:

  • Follow up with you about the ministry, cell group, or membership you registered for
  • Connect you with the right ministry leader or cell group coordinator
  • Respond to any questions or prayer requests you've shared

We do not use your information for marketing, and we never sell it.

Who Has Access

Registration details are stored in our internal church admin system and are only accessible to authorised church staff and ministry leaders who need them to follow up with you. We do not share your information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

How Long We Keep It

We keep registration details for as long as they're useful for staying connected with you as part of the church community. You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time — see "Your Rights" below.

Your Rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Know what information we hold about you
  • Request a copy of it
  • Ask us to correct anything that's inaccurate
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Object to how it's being used

To exercise any of these rights, just reach out using the contact details below.

If You're Under 18

Our Youth ministry welcomes registrants aged 13–17. If you're in this age group, we encourage you to let a parent or guardian know before registering, since we may contact your household directly to coordinate youth activities.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
