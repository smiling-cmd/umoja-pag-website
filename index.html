About Umoja
P.A.G Church

We are a vibrant church in Umoja, Nairobi, dedicated to sharing the love of Christ and building a strong community of faith.

Through worship, prayer, and the Word of God, we create a welcoming space where everyone can grow, connect, and find purpose.

Leadership Spotlight

Pastor John Juma

Faithfully leading Umoja P.A.G Church with a heart for God's people, ministry growth, and helping every member find a place to serve and flourish.

It is my joy and privilege to walk alongside this congregation as we grow together in faith, love, and service to our community. Every person who walks through our doors is family, and every story of transformation reminds me why this calling is worth every sacrifice. My prayer is that Umoja P.A.G Church remains a house of refuge, healing, and hope for generations to come — a place where God's presence is tangible and His people are equipped to go out and make a difference in the world.

Our School

Umoja P.A.G Church School

Our church runs a fully registered primary school offering quality, Christ-centred education from Pre-school through to Class 8. Dedicated in July 2011 by the General Superintendent of P.A.G Kenya, the school provides a nurturing environment where children grow in knowledge, character, and faith.
