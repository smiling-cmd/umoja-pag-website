Our School

Umoja P.A.G Church School

Our church runs a fully registered primary school offering quality, Christ-centred education from Pre-school through to Class 8. Dedicated in July 2011 by the General Superintendent of P.A.G Kenya, the school provides a nurturing environment where children grow in knowledge, character, and faith.
