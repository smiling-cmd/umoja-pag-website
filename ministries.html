Cell Groups

Our Cell Groups meet across Umoja neighbourhoods for midweek fellowship, prayer, Bible study, and mutual care. They are the primary place to build deep relationships and be discipled in community.

Worship Team

The Worship Team leads congregational praise and worship on Sundays. Opportunities include musicians, vocalists, sound techs, and stage support.

Choir

Our Choir ministers through choral worship and special musical presentations. Choir members rehearse weekly and serve on Sunday services and special events.

Media Team

The Media Team supports live streaming, recording, and AV production to ensure the message reaches both the room and online viewers.

Sunday School

Sunday School offers Bible teaching for children and teens, with age-appropriate classes and caring volunteers.

Women's Fellowship

A place for women to gather for prayer, discipleship, and mutual encouragement.

Men's Fellowship

Men gather for fellowship, accountability, and leadership formation.

Youth

Our Youth ministry focuses on mentoring, worship, outreach, and building a strong faith foundation for young people.
