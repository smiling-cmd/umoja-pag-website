Ministries

You belong here. Whatever your gifts, age, or season of life, there's a family below waiting to welcome you in — explore each ministry, meet the leaders, and see where God might be calling you to serve and grow.

Cell Groups

For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them.

Matthew 18:20

Local neighbourhood fellowships that meet weekly for prayer, Bible study and mutual care.

Cell groups are the heartbeat of our church family — smaller circles where you're known by name, not just a face in the crowd. Each group works through the same weekly study so the whole church grows together, while making room for the prayer requests, testimonies, and everyday life of the people in the room.

Neighbourhoods: Jericho, Nazareth, Bethlehem, Judah, Galilee, Jerusalem
What to expect
  • Opening prayer and worship, followed by a guided Bible study
  • Open sharing time — testimonies, prayer requests, practical support
  • Tea/refreshments and fellowship after the study
  • A group leader who follows up with you during the week
MeetsTwice a month
WhereHosted at a cell member's home (varies by neighbourhood)
WhoOpen to all — members and first-time visitors welcome
To joinPick the neighbourhood closest to you and reach out via the How to Join section on the home page; a leader will get in touch

Mary Wanjiru

Cell Groups Coordinator

"Cell groups are where life happens — come as you are; we will pray, study, and grow together."

Worship Team

Serve the LORD with gladness: come before his presence with singing.

Psalm 100:2

Leading the church into God's presence each Sunday through music and worship leadership.

Whether you sing, play an instrument, or run sound from the booth, the worship team exists to help the whole congregation focus on God in every service. New members go through a short onboarding period alongside the team before joining a Sunday rotation, so you're never thrown in unprepared.

What to expect
  • Weekly rehearsal to prepare the set list for the coming Sunday(s)
  • A rotation schedule so serving doesn't clash with your other commitments
  • Mentorship from experienced vocalists and musicians
  • Opportunities to grow in both skill and spiritual leadership
RehearsalsSaturdays, 3:00 PM
ServesRotates across both Sunday services
WhoVocalists, instrumentalists, and sound/tech support
RequirementsA short conversation with the Worship Pastor — no formal audition required

Alex

Worship Pastor

"We aim to lead worship that opens hearts to encounter Jesus — musicians and vocalists are welcome."

Choir

O sing unto the LORD a new song: sing unto the LORD, all the earth.

Psalm 96:1

Choral ministry for special services and congregational encouragement.

The choir ministers through song at special services, festive celebrations, and select Sundays throughout the year — bringing harmony, testimony, and encouragement to the congregation. Members rehearse together to learn parts and build the kind of unity that makes choral singing come alive.

What to expect
  • Weekly rehearsals to learn and practise new pieces
  • Vocal part training (soprano, alto, tenor, bass) for all experience levels
  • Ministering at designated services and special church events
  • A welcoming space to grow your voice and your faith together
RehearsalsAhead of each ministering Sunday — confirm the day with the Choir Director
MinistersSelected Sundays and special services throughout the year
WhoOpen to all voice ranges and experience levels
RequirementsNo prior choir experience needed — just a willing heart to learn

Esther Njeri

Choir Director

"Our choir brings songs of hope and testimony — join us to minister and grow your voice."

Media Team

Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature.

Mark 16:15

Sound, visuals, and streaming — help us communicate the message clearly online and in-house.

From live sound mixing to camera work, lyric slides, and streaming to YouTube and Facebook, the media team makes sure every service is heard and seen clearly — in the sanctuary and by those watching from home. It's hands-on, practical serving that suits anyone who enjoys tech, photography, or design.

What to expect
  • Hands-on training on sound desk, cameras, and streaming software
  • A rotation across roles — sound, visuals/slides, live-stream, photography
  • Arriving early on serving days to set up and test equipment
  • A team environment where technical and creative skills both matter
Setup call time3:00 PM
ServesBoth Sunday services and special events
WhoAnyone interested in sound, video, photography, or streaming
RequirementsNo experience required — training provided

Sunday School

Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.

Proverbs 22:6

Age-appropriate Bible teaching for children and teens delivered by caring volunteers.

Children and teens are split into age-appropriate classes so lessons, songs, and activities always match where they're at. Every teacher and helper is there because they love seeing young people meet Jesus in a way that's fun, safe, and easy for parents to trust during the service.

What to expect
  • Age-graded classes with Bible stories, songs, and simple activities
  • Caring, vetted volunteers leading each class
  • A safe check-in/check-out process for parents
  • Occasional special programs and holiday activities
Classes meetDuring the second Sunday service
WhereMultipurpose Hall
Age groupsSplit by age — toddlers, primary, and teens
WhoChildren and teens of church members and visitors
To volunteerA short interview and basic child-safety training

Women's Fellowship

Strength and honour are her clothing; and she shall rejoice in time to come.

Proverbs 31:25

Prayer, discipleship, and mutual encouragement for women of all ages.

A space where women gather across generations to pray, study the Word, and support one another through the real seasons of life — work, family, singleness, marriage, and everything in between. Expect honest conversation as much as Bible teaching.

What to expect
  • Prayer and worship to open each gathering
  • Bible study or a guest-led topical discussion
  • Mentorship pairing between older and younger women
  • Occasional retreats, seminars, and outreach activities
MeetsTwice a month, weekdays, 5:00 PM — same day as Cell Groups
WhereUmoja P.A.G Church
WhoAll women of the church — no age limit
To joinJust show up, or reach out via the How to Join section on the home page and a leader will follow up

Men's Fellowship

Iron sharpeneth iron; so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend.

Proverbs 27:17

Fellowship, accountability, and leadership formation for men.

Men's Fellowship exists to build men who lead well — at home, at work, and in the church. Meetings combine the Word with honest, practical conversation about the pressures men actually face, and real accountability from other men walking the same road.

What to expect
  • Bible-based teaching on leadership, family, and integrity
  • Open discussion and prayer in small accountability groups
  • Mentorship from older, more established men in the church
  • Occasional service projects and men's outreach events
MeetsTwice a month, weekdays, 5:00 PM — same day as Cell Groups
WhereUmoja P.A.G Church
WhoAll men of the church — no age limit
To joinJust show up, or reach out via the How to Join section on the home page and a leader will follow up

Youth

Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.

1 Timothy 4:12

Mentoring, worship, and outreach for young people as they grow in faith.

Youth service is a space built for young people to bring their real questions, doubts, and excitement about faith — not just sit through a lesson. Between the dedicated 12:30 PM Youth Service, small-group mentoring, and outreach events, the goal is a community that walks with young people, not just teaches at them.

What to expect
  • Youth-focused worship and teaching every Sunday at 12:30 PM
  • Small mentoring groups for deeper connection and accountability
  • Games, outreach events, and camps throughout the year
  • A safe space to ask honest questions about faith and life
MeetsSundays, 12:30 PM Youth Service
Small groupsMeets separately during the week — confirm with a youth leader
WhoTeens and young adults
To joinJust come to the 12:30 PM service, or reach out via the How to Join section on the home page
