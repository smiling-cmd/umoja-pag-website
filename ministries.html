Cell Groups

Local neighbourhood fellowships that meet weekly for prayer, Bible study and mutual care.

Cell groups are the heartbeat of our church family — smaller circles where you're known by name, not just a face in the crowd. Each group works through the same weekly study so the whole church grows together, while making room for the prayer requests, testimonies, and everyday life of the people in the room.

Neighbourhoods: Jericho, Nazareth, Bethlehem, Judah, Galilee, Jerusalem
What to expect
  • Opening prayer and worship, followed by a guided Bible study
  • Open sharing time — testimonies, prayer requests, practical support
  • Tea/refreshments and fellowship after the study
  • A group leader who follows up with you during the week

Worship Team

Leading the church into God's presence each Sunday through music and worship leadership.

Whether you sing, play an instrument, or run sound from the booth, the worship team exists to help the whole congregation focus on God in every service. New members go through a short onboarding period alongside the team before joining a Sunday rotation, so you're never thrown in unprepared.

What to expect
  • Weekly rehearsal to prepare the set list for the coming Sunday(s)
  • A rotation schedule so serving doesn't clash with your other commitments
  • Mentorship from experienced vocalists and musicians
  • Opportunities to grow in both skill and spiritual leadership

Choir

Choral ministry for special services and congregational encouragement.

The choir ministers through song at special services, festive celebrations, and select Sundays throughout the year — bringing harmony, testimony, and encouragement to the congregation. Members rehearse together to learn parts and build the kind of unity that makes choral singing come alive.

What to expect
  • Weekly rehearsals to learn and practise new pieces
  • Vocal part training (soprano, alto, tenor, bass) for all experience levels
  • Ministering at designated services and special church events
  • A welcoming space to grow your voice and your faith together

Media Team

Sound, visuals, and streaming — help us communicate the message clearly online and in-house.

From live sound mixing to camera work, lyric slides, and streaming to YouTube and Facebook, the media team makes sure every service is heard and seen clearly — in the sanctuary and by those watching from home. It's hands-on, practical serving that suits anyone who enjoys tech, photography, or design.

What to expect
  • Hands-on training on sound desk, cameras, and streaming software
  • A rotation across roles — sound, visuals/slides, live-stream, photography
  • Arriving early on serving days to set up and test equipment
  • A team environment where technical and creative skills both matter

Youth

Mentoring, worship, and outreach for young people as they grow in faith.

Youth service is a space built for young people to bring their real questions, doubts, and excitement about faith — not just sit through a lesson. Between the dedicated 12:30 PM Youth Service, small-group mentoring, and outreach events, the goal is a community that walks with young people, not just teaches at them.

What to expect
  • Youth-focused worship and teaching every Sunday at 12:30 PM
  • Small mentoring groups for deeper connection and accountability
  • Games, outreach events, and camps throughout the year
  • A safe space to ask honest questions about faith and life
